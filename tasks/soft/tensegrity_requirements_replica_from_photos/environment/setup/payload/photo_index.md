# Photo index

`photos/photo_001.jpg` to `photos/photo_320.jpg` show one strut of the robot. Its net of cables and strain sensors had
been cut away and the loose leads between its parts taken off before the first photo: none of them is in any photo.
The made parts carry paper tags, P01 to P13; where parts are laid out on the mat, each tag lies beside its part.

## Camera

One hand-held phone took all photos with three lenses whose focal lengths are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 5 (1x, 2x and 5x in
the table). The camera is not calibrated and no camera pose was recorded. Every photo is tilted and rolled: none looks
square onto the mat or onto a part. Exposure and white balance vary a little, and file names do not encode the pose.

## Scale references

Nominal sizes of the scale references. The last column of the table names the references that lay on the mat in a
scene; a close photo has only some of them in the frame.

- **mat**: cutting mat, A3 (420 x 297 mm), 2 mm thick, with a 10 mm grid whose every fifth line is bold and centimetre
  numbers along two margins. The parts rest on its top face.
- **checker card**: 10 x 7 squares of 12 mm inside a 6 mm white border, 132 x 96 mm, 1 mm thick.
- **small card**: 10 x 7 squares of 2 mm inside a 3 mm white border, 26 x 20 mm, 1 mm thick.
- **standing small card**: a small card standing on its long edge against a plain block (30 x 14 x 24 mm), leaning
  back 8 deg.
- **rule**: steel rule, 160 x 18 mm, 0.5 mm thick, with a 150 mm scale graduated in millimetres (numbered every
  centimetre) on one edge and in half millimetres on the other.
- **long rule**: steel rule, 320 x 25 mm, 1 mm thick, with a 300 mm scale graduated the same way.
- **paper tags**: 30 x 14 mm.

on only to tell them apart in this table. Where a row holds photos of more than one lens, the lens column names the
