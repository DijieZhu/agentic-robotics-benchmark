# Incident note: vehicle held against the trash rack

From the pilot of the rented vehicle, for the Owner's file of the previous inspection campaign.

**What happened.** Dive 06 was to continue the strips on the face of the trash rack. While the vehicle worked close
to the rack it lost its standoff, was drawn onto the bars by the flow through the rack and was held against them.
The pilot backed it off at full astern thrust.

**Outcome.** The dive was abandoned and the vehicle was recovered. Damage: scuff marks on the front of the frame,
nothing else. The thrusters, the camera housing and the tether termination were checked after recovery and found in
order.

**Records.** Dive 06 gave no survey record. It is not entered in the dive log, and its telemetry was not kept.
