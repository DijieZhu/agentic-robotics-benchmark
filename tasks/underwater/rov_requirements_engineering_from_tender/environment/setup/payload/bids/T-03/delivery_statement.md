# Delivery statement

- Tender: TS-01 rev C
- Bid: T-03
- Bidder: Northmere Survey

This statement lists our test facilities and the units we can deliver, for the acceptance under Part D.

## 1. Test facilities

Facilities at our works:

| Facility | Range |
|---|---|
| Trim tank | 4.7 m long, 2.8 m wide, 2.1 m deep |
| Trim tank water | harbour water from the harbour main, filled for each weighing; the density is logged at each weighing and lies between 1005.0 and 1023.0 kg/m3 |
| Bollard pool, bench supply at the battery interface | 11 V to 17 V |
| Climatic chamber | 10 degC to 35 degC |
| Battery cycler, constant-power load | 60 W to 720 W |
| Pressure vessel | up to 30 bar, held for up to 4 h |
| Tow tank, carriage speed | 0.2 m/s to 1.0 m/s |

## 2. Delivery band

We can deliver any unit within these ranges:

| Item | Range |
|---|---|
| Ballast pieces fitted at the works | 0 to 10 removable pieces. Our works practice is the number fitted to the tendered unit, 6 (see TR-1). |
| Battery pack | Stock grade A, B or C of the pack fitted. For the tendered pack the catalogue codes are PK-18 for grade A and PK-18/B for grades B and C. If an option changes the pack, the grades are those of that pack, coded the same way. |
| Factory current preset | 24 / 54 / 66 / 72 A |
| Tether | Cut from the drum to the length ordered, within the length tolerance of the catalogue tether sheet. Made-up lengths in stock, 6.8 mm x 150 m: 148.5 / 149.2 / 150.0 / 150.7 / 151.5 m; 6.8 mm x 200 m: 198.0 / 199.0 / 200.0 / 201.0 / 202.0 m. |

Any ballast number can be combined with any grade, any preset and any made-up tether length.

## 3. Order of preference

Where the acceptance schedule leaves a choice among the units of section 2, we deliver in this order of preference. An earlier item decides before a later.

(a) Ballast: the number of pieces nearest to our works practice; of numbers equally near, the smaller.
(b) Battery pack: grade C before grade B, and grade B before grade A.
(c) Current preset: the lowest.
(d) Tether: the made-up length listed first in section 2, then the length after it.

A copy of this statement is kept in our files.
