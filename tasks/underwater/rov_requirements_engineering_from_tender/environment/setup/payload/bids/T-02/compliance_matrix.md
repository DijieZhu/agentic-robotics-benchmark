## 3. Notes

The declared value of each line follows from the document cited in the last column of the matrix. Where a line cites a catalogue page and a factory test report, the catalogue page gives the rating and the report records the test. Pressure ratings are quoted from the catalogue series pages of the pressure housings. The parts tested, with their proof pressures, are recorded on TR-3.
