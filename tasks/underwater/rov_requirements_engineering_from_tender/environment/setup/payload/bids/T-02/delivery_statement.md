# Delivery statement

- Tender: TS-01 rev C
- Bid: T-02
- Bidder: Calderwick Systems

This statement describes the test facilities of our works and the range of units we are able to deliver, for the purposes of the acceptance under Part D.

## 1. Test facilities

The following facilities are available for acceptance testing.

| Facility | Range |
|---|---|
| Trim tank | 6.3 m long, 3.1 m wide, 2.8 m deep |
| Trim tank water | fresh water, held at 20.0 degC by the tank heater; the tank is topped up, not refilled, between weighings |
| Bollard pool, bench supply at the battery interface | 11 V to 17 V |
| Climatic chamber | 15 degC to 35 degC |
| Battery cycler, constant-power load | 60 W to 720 W |
| Pressure vessel | up to 30 bar, held for up to 4 h |
| Tow tank, carriage speed | 0.2 m/s to 1.0 m/s |

## 2. Delivery band

Within the following ranges we are able to deliver any unit.

| Item | Range |
|---|---|
| Ballast pieces fitted at the works | 0 to 10 removable pieces. The works practice is the number fitted to the tendered unit, which is 4 and is recorded on TR-1. |
| Battery pack | Stock grade A, B or C of the battery pack fitted. For the tendered pack the catalogue codes of the grades are PK-18, PK-18/B and PK-18/Cx respectively. Where an option changes the pack, the grades are those of the optional pack and are coded in the same way. |
| Factory current preset | 42 / 54 / 66 / 72 A |
| Tether | Cut from the drum to the length ordered, within the length tolerance of the catalogue tether sheet. Made-up lengths in stock, 6.8 mm x 200 m: 198.0 / 199.0 / 200.0 / 201.0 / 202.0 m. |

The ranges of the table, the made-up tether lengths included, may be combined without restriction.

## 3. Order of preference

Where the acceptance schedule admits more than a single unit of section 2, the unit delivered is chosen by the following order of convenience. An earlier item takes precedence over a later.

(a) Ballast: the number of pieces nearest to the works practice and, between numbers equally near, the smaller.
(b) Battery pack: stock grade C in preference to B, and B in preference to A.
(c) Current preset: the lowest available.
(d) Tether: the made-up length that stands first in the list of section 2, then the following ones in turn.
