# Delivery statement

- Tender: TS-01 rev C
- Bid: T-07
- Bidder: Veltmann Subsea Instruments

Our works test facilities and the units we are able to deliver, for the acceptance under Part D.

## 1. Test facilities

Test facilities:

| Facility | Range |
|---|---|
| Trim tank | 7.2 m long, 3.4 m wide, 3.3 m deep |
| Bollard pool, bench supply at the battery interface | 11 V to 17 V |
| Climatic chamber | 10 degC to 35 degC |
| Battery cycler, constant-power load | 60 W to 720 W |
| Pressure vessel | up to 30 bar, held for up to 4 h |
| Tow tank, carriage speed | 0.2 m/s to 1.0 m/s |

## 2. Delivery band

Any unit within the following ranges can be delivered.

| Item | Range |
|---|---|
| Ballast pieces fitted at the works | 0 to 10 removable pieces. Works practice is the number fitted to the tendered unit: 4, as recorded on TR-1. |
| Battery pack | Stock grade A, B or C of the battery pack fitted. For the tendered pack the catalogue codes are PK-18, PK-18/B and PK-18/C. Were an option to change the pack, the grades would be those of that pack, coded in the same way. |
| Factory current preset | 42 / 54 / 66 / 72 A |

The ranges are independent and can be combined freely.

## 3. Order of preference

Where the acceptance schedule leaves a choice among the units of section 2, the unit is selected in the following order. An earlier item prevails over a later.

(a) Ballast: the number nearest to works practice; between numbers equally near, the smaller.
(b) Battery pack: grade C before grade B, grade B before grade A.
(c) Current preset: the lowest.
