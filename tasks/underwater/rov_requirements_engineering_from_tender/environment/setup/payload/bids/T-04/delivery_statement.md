# Delivery statement

- Tender: TS-01 rev C
- Bid: T-04
- Bidder: Marlstone Harbour Services

Test facilities and delivery band, for the acceptance under Part D.

## 1. Test facilities

Facilities available for acceptance tests:

| Facility | Range |
|---|---|
| Trim tank | 4.3 m long, 2.9 m wide, 1.9 m deep |
| Trim tank water | harbour water from the harbour main, filled for each weighing; the density is logged at each weighing and lies between 1005.0 and 1023.0 kg/m3 |
| Bollard pool, bench supply at the battery interface | 11 V to 17 V |
| Climatic chamber | 15 degC to 35 degC |
| Battery cycler, constant-power load | 60 W to 720 W |
| Pressure vessel | up to 30 bar, held for up to 4 h |
| Tow tank, carriage speed | 0.2 m/s to 1.0 m/s |

## 2. Delivery band

Units we can deliver:

| Item | Range |
|---|---|
| Ballast pieces fitted at the works | 0 to 10 removable pieces. Works practice: as the tendered unit, 4 pieces (TR-1). |
| Battery pack | Stock grade A, B or C of the pack fitted. Catalogue codes for the tendered pack: PK-15E, PK-15E/B, PK-15E/C. With option O-41 the codes are PK-18, PK-18/B and PK-18/C. |
| Factory current preset | 42 / 54 / 66 / 72 A |
| Tether | Cut from the drum to the length ordered, within the length tolerance of the catalogue tether sheet. Made-up lengths in stock, 6.8 mm x 115 m: 113.8 / 114.4 / 115.0 / 115.6 / 116.2 m; 6.8 mm x 150 m: 148.5 / 149.2 / 150.0 / 150.7 / 151.5 m. |

The ranges, the made-up tether lengths included, combine freely.

## 3. Order of preference

Where the acceptance schedule allows more than a single unit, we deliver in this order of convenience. An earlier item takes precedence over a later.

(a) Ballast: nearest to works practice; of equally near numbers, the smaller.
(b) Battery pack: grade C, then B, then A.
(c) Current preset: lowest first.
(d) Tether: made-up lengths in the order of section 2.
