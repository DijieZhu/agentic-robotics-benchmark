THE REGISTERED RULE OF THE GRADED LINE SET (written before any draw). Eight shifts; slots: 1 slow (0.05-0.15 m/s) running, 2 medium (0.15-0.25) running, 3 fast (0.25-0.35) running, 4 medium indexing, 5 fast running, 6 slow indexing, 7 medium running, 8 fast running. One salt of 16 bytes from the system's entropy, drawn once at selection time and recorded in the hidden selection file; each slot's shift is the frozen line generator's draw for the seed hash(salt, slot label, 0): the first draw, with no quota and no admission; nothing is rejected and nothing is run on a candidate before the set is frozen. After the freeze, as information only: the reference's and two other programs' scores on the set.

How it is carried out. The slots in order, each with its belt class and belt mode: 1 slow running, 2 medium running, 3 fast running, 4 medium indexing, 5 fast running, 6 slow indexing, 7 medium running, 8 fast running (review/gen_shifts.py LINE_SLOTS; the belt classes of grader_config.json block line: slow 0.05-0.15 m/s, medium 0.15-0.25 m/s, fast 0.25-0.35 m/s, the speed drawn uniformly inside the class and rounded to 1 mm/s; indexing: the belt stands while the station's parcel has its centre in y -0.15..0.10). The salt: os.urandom(16), called once by review/draw_line_set.py draw, recorded as salt_hex in hidden/line/selection.json. Slot s's label is 'line-hidden-<s>' and its seed parcelcell.shiftgen.line_seed('line-hidden-<s>', 0, cfg, mix=salt): the first 8 bytes, shifted right by one bit, of sha256 of '<task id>/line-hidden-<s>/<line shifts version>/0' followed by '/' and the salt. Slot s's shift is parcelcell.shiftgen.draw_line_shift(seed, cfg, profile='full', belt_class, indexing) on the files pinned by tests/frozen.json (review/freeze.py check passes before the draw), written as hidden/line/shifts/line_<ss>.json with its sha256 in hidden/line/seeds.json; review/draw_line_set.py check draws the set again from the recorded salt and compares the bytes. The rule file's sha256 is recorded in hidden/line/selection.json and in the maintainer's log before the draw.
