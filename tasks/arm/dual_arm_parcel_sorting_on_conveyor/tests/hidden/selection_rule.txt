THE REGISTERED RULE OF THE HIDDEN SET (written before any draw)

Eight shifts; slot order and belt class: 1 slow (0.05-0.13 m/s), 2 medium (0.13-0.21), 3 fast (0.21-0.28), 4 medium,
5 fast, 6 slow, 7 medium, 8 fast. One salt of 16 bytes from the system's entropy, drawn once at selection time and
recorded in the hidden selection file; a candidate's seed is the hash of salt, slot label and a counter k = 0, 1, 2,
... The frozen generator draws each candidate. A slot takes the FIRST candidate that meets all of: (q1) at least 6
in-duty parcels arriving label not up, of which at least 3 with the label underneath and at least 2 with the label on
the downstream face; (q2) at least 2 cartons arriving label-up; (q3) at least 2 mailers; (q4) at least 3 cartons
outside the duty; (q5) the natural end at most 600 s (true of every generated shift); (q6) at least 2 in-duty cartons
whose length along the belt is more than 0.26 m; (a) the frozen reference, through the privileged path on the task's
own cell with the task's numerics settings, leaves every in-duty parcel and every label-up parcel readable at the scan
line, loses or damages nothing, and has no protective stop and no limiter clip; (b) a second run in a fresh process
gives the identical record. Every rejected candidate is recorded with its reason. Nothing else may influence the
choice; no candidate is run with a camera-driven program before the set is frozen. The anchor is the reference's
reward in one fresh full verify in the image.

Readings, fixed before the draw:
  (q1)  an in-duty parcel arriving label not up is a parcel of class W (a carton whose label is underneath or on the
        downstream face as it lands, its label more than 25 degrees from vertical up);
  (q2)  a carton arriving label-up is a carton of class U;
  (q4)  a carton outside the duty is a carton of class X (its label on the upstream, the near side or the far side
        face as it lands);
  (q5)  every full shift lasts the same fixed time, 600 s, whatever its belt speed and arrivals, so (q5) is read as
        the shift's natural end being at most 600 s: last arrival + 2.1 m / belt speed + 40 s (the last parcel's ride
        from the entry line past the scan line, and the tail), computed from the arrivals and the belt speed, never
        from the package's duration field; the frozen generator draws only shifts that meet it; the check stays part
        of the rule;
  (q6)  an in-duty carton is a parcel of class W; its length along the belt is the side that lies along the belt as
        it lands (the landing yaw is a quarter turn k x 90 degrees within the yaw tolerance: the carton's second side
        for even k, its first side for odd k); it counts when that length is more than 0.26 m.

How it is carried out (tests/review/select_hidden.py, inside the task image, on the frozen files that
tests/frozen.json pins):
  slot label   "hidden-<slot>" (slots 1 to 8);
  seed         parcelcell.shiftgen.labelled_seed("hidden-<slot>", k, mix=salt): the first 8 bytes (shifted right by
               one bit) of sha256 over the text "<task id>/hidden-<slot>/<shifts version>/<k>", a "/" and the
               salt's 16 bytes;
  candidate    parcelcell.shiftgen.draw_shift(seed, config, profile "full", the slot's belt class);
  quotas       (q1)-(q6) from the candidate's parcels alone (no reference run for a candidate that misses one);
  (a) and (b)  two runs of the reference, each in a fresh process with the grader's numerics settings, as the grader
               drives the oracle's program: describe(), one frame of every camera, reference_run(), finish(); (b)
               compares the shift's record without its wall-clock and render-timing fields, the final physics state
               digest and the limiter count;
  record       tests/hidden/selection.json: the salt, this file's sha256, the sha256 of every frozen file before and
               after the selection, every candidate with its class counts, natural end, quota result, run digests,
               admission result and reasons; tests/hidden/seeds.json and tests/hidden/shifts/ hold the chosen eight.
