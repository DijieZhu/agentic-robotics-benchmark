At build time parcelcell attaches this model twice (prefixes L_ and R_) to the
wrist flange sites, drops its keyframes and, by default, softens the four finger
pad boxes (solref 0.02 1, sliding friction 1.8) to model compliant pad covers.
The file itself is not edited.
