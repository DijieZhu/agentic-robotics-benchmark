# NOTICE - sources

- **The robot** is the Franka Panda model of robosuite 1.5.2 (MIT), installed unmodified in the task image; the
  cable jaws on its fingers are this task's.
- **Everything else in the cell** (the bench, the board and its print, the clips, the posts, the connector, the
  cable, the reels, bins and tape) is built from MuJoCo primitives and generated meshes; no third-party assets.
- **The hall in the cameras' pictures** (walls, floor, racks, tool chests, a cart, pallets, a conveyor, a forklift)
  is part of the Unity Asset Store pack "Factory Warehouse", simplified; its light was measured in a path-traced
  render of that hall under Poly Haven's `aerodynamics_workshop` panorama (CC0).
