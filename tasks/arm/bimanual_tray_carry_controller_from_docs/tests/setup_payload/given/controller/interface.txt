THE CONTROLLER'S INTERFACE

CALLS. submission/controller/controller.py defines class Controller, built with no argument (Controller()), with two calls:
  setup(unit_record, site) -> {"gripper_mode": {"left": m, "right": m}}, m one of "mit", "posforce".
    Called once per unit, before its shift, with the modules disabled. unit_record is that unit's delivery record (the format of given/unit/record_format.txt). site = {"cell": the unit's cell sheet as data (the format of given/site/cell_sheet.json: the drawn poses of F and S with their tolerances, the ambient band), "shift": the unit's shift sheet (the format of given/site/shift_format.txt)}. Budget 60 s CPU, 120 s wall. Anything but a valid mode choice, an exception or a budget exceeded fails that unit's gate. Of setup's result only "gripper_mode" is read; other keys are ignored. A module's PMAX, VMAX and TMAX are rows of its register_dump in unit_record.
    The supervisor then writes each gripper module's control-mode register to the returned mode (a mode can be written only while a module is disabled; the mode holds for the whole shift), enables the sixteen modules and holds the record's home pose with the maker's driver law at its shipped gains for 2.0 s (joint PD, no feed-forward), commanded through the unit's own stored zeros.
  step(feedback) -> {"frames": [f0 .. f15], "decline": bool} ("decline" may be left out: false), every 4 ms from the hand-over to the end of the shift. Budget 4 ms CPU per call. From cycle 0 the controller's frames replace the driver's; the first tray is presented 8.0 s later.
  One process per unit, started fresh; a controller keeps what it likes in memory across the calls of one unit and nothing across units. Importing controller.py and building Controller() must finish within 60 s of wall time; otherwise the controller is not started for that unit and that unit's gate fails.
  The process: its working directory is a fresh copy of the folder submission/controller/, read-only, first on the import path; HOME and TMPDIR are fresh, empty, writable folders; Python is started with -B -E -s (no bytecode written, Python's environment variables such as PYTHONPATH and the user's site folder ignored); OMP_NUM_THREADS, OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS and MKL_NUM_THREADS are 1; OPENBLAS_CORETYPE is "Haswell" and OPENBLAS_L2_SIZE is 256 (the numeric library's kernel and the cache size it assumes); MUJOCO_GL is "disable" (the simulator's package imports and runs; its rendering is switched off). The process stays the reaper of what it starts: its orphaned descendants pass to it (it is their subreaper), the child signal (SIGCHLD) keeps its default action, and in the process and in every process it starts an attempt to set that signal's action or the subreaper setting (prctl PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER) fails with an error (EPERM; Python's signal.signal raises PermissionError), so the wait builtin of /bin/sh (dash) for a job started in the background that is still running when the wait begins does not return and multiprocessing's forkserver start method fails. There is no network; every thread and process it starts is charged to its CPU.
  The process's standard output is the wire: every line on it is read, in order, as the answer to the call in progress. print() goes to the process's log (standard error). A line that the controller writes to its standard output itself is therefore read as an answer: one that is not of the wire's own form ends the controller; one that is takes the place of the answer to the call in progress, and every later answer then answers the call after its own.

THE EXCHANGE. The host cycle is 4 ms. Cycle k: the controller receives the replies sampled at the start of cycle k - 1 and returns the frames of cycle k, which the modules receive at the start of cycle k and hold until replaced. Feedback is therefore exactly one cycle old. There is no loss and no burst. Simulated time does not move while a call computes.

FRAMES. Module order: left J1..J7, left gripper, right J1..J7, right gripper. All angles are module angles (the unit's own stored zero; module angle and model joint angle have the same sign on every joint; gripper 0 = closed).
  MIT frame (the fourteen arm modules; a gripper in "mit" mode): {"p": rad, "v": rad/s, "kp": N m/rad, "kd": N m s/rad, "t": N m}, exactly these keys. Each field is clipped to its range and quantised by truncation as the real frame does: p over +-12.5 rad in 16 bits (0.0003815 rad); v over +-VMAX in 12 bits (VMAX DM8009 45, DM4340 10, DM4310 30 rad/s: steps 0.02198 / 0.004884 / 0.01465 rad/s); kp over [0, 500] in 12 bits (0.1221); kd over [0, 5] in 12 bits (0.001221); t over +-TMAX in 12 bits (TMAX DM8009 54, DM4340 28, DM4310 10 N m: steps 0.02637 / 0.01368 / 0.004884 N m). The ranges used are the module's registers PMAX, VMAX and TMAX, printed in its register dump in the unit's record.
  Force-position frame (a gripper in "posforce" mode): {"p": rad, "v_lim": rad/s, "i_lim": per unit}, exactly these keys: p as float32; v_lim over [0, 100] rad/s in steps of 0.01; i_lim over [0, 1] in steps of 1e-04, a fraction of that module's maximum drive current as its record prints it.
  null in a module's place: no frame this cycle; the module keeps executing its last command.
  Every number of a frame is a finite JSON number, not a boolean. A frame of the wrong kind for a module's mode reaches the module, which ignores it without a reply, as the real module does (its age then grows). Of a reply only "frames" and "decline" are read; other top-level keys are ignored. A reply that is an object but not a valid reply (a frames list of a wrong length, a frame whose keys are not exactly those of its kind, a field that is not a finite number, a decline that is not a boolean) counts as a late cycle, and its decline is ignored. Frames are never rejected for their values: a legal frame is whatever passes the clip.

THE GRIPPER'S TWO MODES. "mit": the same law as an arm joint (torque = kp (p - theta) + kd (v - w) + t, limited by the drive). "posforce": the vendor's force-position mode: the module moves towards p no faster than v_lim and with its current held below i_lim; the torque it then commands against an obstacle (its plateau, in the drive's own units, which its reply reports) is i_lim x the module's maximum drive current x its torque per ampere: the maximum drive current as read at this unit's power-up, the torque per ampere by the vendor's printed formula KT = 1.5 x pole pairs x flux x gear ratio x GREF from the module's own registers in the record's full dump. The drive holds its current at OC_Value x the module's maximum drive current and does not trip there (on these units the over-current protection of the bus page never acts; the coil protection does), so the plateau is never above OC_Value x that current x KT whatever i_lim is. In either mode the gripper's joint receives no more than the module's peak torque (7 N m on the DM-J4310 V1.1) times that module's delivered-torque factor (DELIVERED TORQUE in given/integrator/development_units.txt); the reply's torque, the drive's own figure, is not held to the peak torque and can read up to OC_Value x the maximum drive current x KT. The mode is chosen per hand in setup and is the register it is: it cannot be changed during a shift.

FEEDBACK. {"cycle": k, "site": {"tray": i or null, "slot_cycle": n or null}, "modules": [m0 .. m15]}. Each m: "q" (rad, 16 bit), "dq" (rad/s, 12 bit), "tau" (N m, 12 bit: the drive's own torque value, computed from its current and its torque constant), each decoded with the module's own PMAX, VMAX and TMAX; "t_mos" and "t_rotor" (integer deg C); "status" (the reply's status code, sent as an integer: 1 enabled, 0 disabled, 9 under voltage, and a letter code of the module pages as its hexadecimal value: C as 12, D as 13); "age" (cycles since that module's last reply was sampled: 1 unless the controller was late or the module got no frame of its kind). site is the supervisor's signal: the index of the slot last presented and the number of cycles since that presentation, both null before the first presentation. Nothing else is on the wire: no tray pose, no force, no camera, no clock other than cycle.

THE DECLINE. `"decline": true` in a reply sent inside the present tray's decision window declines that tray: from that cycle the supervisor follows the declined tray's schedule of the shift sheet. Outside the window the field is ignored. It is also ignored after a missed capture: when a hand had not captured its neck at t = 5.0 and no decline had been sent before t = 5.0. A decline counts in J only if both hands captured the tray in that slot before the reply was sent and the tray then stands released in its fixture at the put-back deadline, its four feet on the fixture's plate inside their cups.

BUDGETS AND WHAT A LATE STEP DOES. A step that uses more than 4 ms of CPU is late by floor(cpu / 4 ms) whole cycles L: its frames are not sent; for those L cycles the modules receive nothing and keep their last command; the controller is next called L cycles later, with feedback whose age is L + 1, and a decline in the late reply reaches the supervisor then. So a reply late by one cycle is followed by a call at the next cycle, and the lateness shows only in the ages, which then read 2. A step above 1 s of CPU or 5 s of wall, an exception, a reply that is not an object or a reply line above 1 MiB (more than 1048576 bytes before its newline) ends the controller for that unit: the modules keep their last command, the shift runs on without it until an event or its end, and what was not done scores 0. Gate: at least 90 per cent of a unit's cycles in which the controller ran on time; a shift whose late cycles exceed 10 per cent of the planned shift is stopped there.

ENABLE AND DISABLE. Both belong to the supervisor. The controller sends control frames only: it can neither enable, disable, clear an error, save a zero nor write a register. A module that leaves enable through one of its protections stays out, and that is an event. At the end of the shift the supervisor takes the pair back.

THE TOY SELF-TEST. check_controller.py runs a controller against toy_chain.py: two modules in a planar chain and one finger pair with made-up masses, the same frame codec, the 4 ms exchange, one-cycle-old feedback, the late-step rule and the site signal with a two-slot toy shift (python3 check_controller.py [path/to/controller.py]). It checks the protocol and the timing and prints the budgets used. It is not the robot, holds no value of any unit, and its pass says nothing about a score. example_controller.py holds the hand-over pose and never declines.
