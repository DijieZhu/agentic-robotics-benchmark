INTEGRATOR'S SHEET: WHAT WE OBSERVED ON OUR DEVELOPMENT UNITS

Our development units (six pairs of OpenArm 2.0 on the maker's pedestal, zeroed with the maker's routine and
passed on our fixture) were measured as described below. For every quantity in which a unit differs from the
maker's files the range observed over those units is given. Customer units were not measured individually.

JOINT FRICTION IN MOTION (Coulomb level, per joint, both arms; N m at the joint):
  J1: 0.306 to 1.02 N m
  J2: 0.306 to 0.72 N m
  J3: 0.2 to 0.586 N m
  J4: 0.15 to 1.27 N m
  J5: 0.05 to 0.16 N m
  J6: 0.09 to 0.16 N m
  J7: 0.1 to 0.31 N m
  Observed by slow constant-speed moves in both directions on the free arm. A held joint rests anywhere
  inside its band.
  Along a joint's travel (each of J1 to J7, both arms) the level is not constant: it varies about the unit's
  level by up to about +-57 per cent (29 per cent RMS), in a pattern that repeats every 1.7 to 2.3 deg of joint angle
  and is fixed for a unit; the levels above are averages over a stroke.

VISCOUS DAMPING (per joint, N m s/rad at the joint):
  J1-J2: 0 to 0.52;  J3-J4: 0 to 0.81;  J5-J7: 0.015 to 0.1
  Observed from the same moves at two speeds.

DELIVERED TORQUE: a module delivers between about 12 per cent less and 4 per cent more than it commands
  (each module on its own; observed against a reference load at the output, every module of every unit).

CONSTANT TORQUE OFFSETS (per joint, N m): J1-J4 within +-0.15, J5-J7 within +-0.04.

ZERO RESIDUAL of a unit that passed the fixture check: every arm joint within +-0.4 deg.
MOUNTING of the right arm against the left: within +-0.5 mm per axis and +-0.15 deg about each axis.

GRIPPER BLOCK:
  gripper friction (per hand, N m at the finger drive): 0.20 to 0.92 N m.
  gripper viscous damping (per hand, at the finger drive, both fingers together): 0.084 N m s/rad on every unit.
  pad friction on the necks' grip blocks: 0.55 or more.
  On development units, with a load cell between the pads: the clamp rises as (the delivered torque less
  the finger's friction) / 0.067 m; it does not change while the delivered torque is lowered by less than
  twice that friction; lowered further, it follows (the delivered torque plus the finger's friction) / 0.067 m.

REPLIES: a module's reply reaches the host 1 cycle old.

SITES: the lab ambient of our customers' sites lies between 20 and 30 deg C. At the
hand-over each module's coil reads between the ambient and 15 K above it.

COILS: holds against a fixed stop at standstill, on development units, each module class at several commanded
torques, from rooms of 20 to 30 deg C with the coil between the room temperature and 15 K above it.
Each row is min-to-max over the holds made on those units; the coil protection was set to 100 deg C (OT_Value) on every module.
'time to protection' is the time from the start of the hold to the coil protection ('none in 30 min'
when none came); 'reply at protection' is what both temperature fields of the module's reply (rotor and
driver) read at that moment; 'reply, next 60 s' is the highest they read in the following minute of cool-down;
'clearable after' is the time after the protection at which the module accepted a clear.
