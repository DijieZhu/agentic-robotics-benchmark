STAGE 05 (OpenArm pair, tray-carry controller): THE BAND RULE
Plain text of rules/band_rule.json; the two say the same. Pre-registered before any number of the built
task exists. No number of any built unit, shift, tray or submission appears here; the floors are tolerances or one field step
at the observable, computed on the maker's nominal model.
The pre-check that section 8 quotes was committed before this rule.

1. CREDIT (linear, as #88)
   For a quantity x of one row (smaller is better), with F the full end and Z the zero end:
     L(x) = 1 if x <= F; 0 if x >= Z; (Z - x) / (Z - F) in between.

2. FULL END
   F_x = max(floor_x, 1.25 x the 90th percentile of x over the privileged reference's rows on the three true units, pooled).
   A floor is what a printed tolerance or a field's resolution produces at the scored point, with one line of derivation; it is
   never a measurement of what the agent's material allows. A floor that nothing printed gives is listed as
   going back to the maintainers (section 6) and is not invented.

3. ZERO END
   Z_x = the median of x over the rows of the reference with the owning factor's ONE belief set to the documents' value (the
   named mutant of section 6), on the three true units. Recorded beside it, never used as an end: the medians with that
   belief's error (documents' value minus truth) scaled by 0.25, 0.5 and 2.

4. THE 3x RULE
   A quantity whose Z_x is below 3 F_x is merged into another quantity of the same owner or reported only. Merged: its credit
   factor is dropped and the term is computed from the owner's remaining quantity (H keeps the other of e_z and theta; place
   keeps the remaining of v_c, P_r, d_s). A term left with no scored quantity is reported only and its weight goes to the
   remaining terms of Q in proportion; a place term with no quantity left leaves the product (tray = completed x Q). Decided
   once, on the frozen set, by this rule; never by looking at a model's episode.

5. STRUCTURE AND WEIGHTS OF RECORD
     Q_i     = 0.30 H + 0.25 R + 0.25 A + 0.20 P        each term the mean of its rows over tray i's job
     H row   = L(e_z) x L(theta)                         one row per hold
     P row   = L(d_m) x progress_m                       one row per move; progress = travel projected on the move, in [0, 1]
     place_i = L(v_c) x L(P_r)                           (x L(d_s) only if d_s passes the 3x rule)
     tray_i  = completed_i x Q_i x (0.5 + 0.5 x place_i)
     unit_u  = 0.85 x the mean over the unit's CARRY trays of tray_i + 0.15 x J_u
     raw     = gates x the mean over the three units of unit_u;   reward = min(1, raw / anchor)
   completed_i = 1 iff captured, carried without an event, standing on S inside its seat frame (the seat mark grown by 10 mm on
   the true shelf, all four feet) with both grippers open at T_e + 6.0. A carry tray that is declined, missed at capture, cut by
   an event or never reached has tray_i = 0 and stays in the mean. J_u = max(0, TPR - FPR) over the unit's decline and carry
   trays; a tray counts as declined only if it stands released in F at its put-back deadline; a tray never reached counts as
   not declined. The anchor is the privileged reference's raw score on the frozen set (tests/anchor.json).
   When a term leaves Q (3x rule or a pre-check line) the remaining Q weights are divided by their sum. The unit's 0.85 / 0.15
   and the tray's 0.5 / 0.5 change only as section 8 says (J at 0.10 if line a1 fails: 0.90 / 0.10).

6. TERMS, QUANTITIES, WINDOWS, OWNERS, ZERO-END MUTANTS, FLOORS
   H, hold (0.30). Owner: the load is in no model (mass and centre). Mutant M-load: the reference feeding forward the middle
     of the printed band (1.25 kg) with its centre midway. Window: the mean over the last 0.4 s of each 0.9 s hold.
     e_z, height error of the tray reference point, mm. Floor 0.12 mm: one position step (25 / 65535 rad = 0.021857 deg) at
       each of the fourteen arm joints through the nominal kinematics, vertical shift of the reference point, root-sum-square,
       median 0.1219 mm over the box (0.099 to 0.138).
     theta, deck angle from level, deg. Floor 0.047 deg: one position step at each of the fourteen joints, the grip points'
       vertical difference over 0.360 m and the hands' mean rotation about the handle line, root-sum-square, median 0.0466 deg
       (0.041 to 0.051).
   R, repeatability (0.25). Owner: joint friction (level by direction, holding band, profile along the travel). Mutant M-fric:
     the reference with the model file's joint table (frictionloss 0.2 / 0.1 / 0.04, damping 1.0 / 0.9 / 0.9). Window: the
     hold windows of targets visited at least twice from directions at least 60 deg apart.
     rho_t, the largest distance between a target's settled positions over its visits, mm. Floor 0.28 mm: two settled poses,
       each with one position step per joint: sqrt(2) x 0.2009 mm (the reference point's 3-D shift per step, root-sum-square,
       median over the box) = 0.284 mm.
   A, absolute position (0.25). Owner: zero residual and mounting, against the site's datum. Mutant M-geom: zeros of 0, the
     drawn mounting, the tray placed by the nominal kinematics (the capture in F not used). Window: the hold windows.
     e_xy, horizontal distance of the reference point from its target in the cell frame, mm. Floor 0.33 mm: the 90th
       percentile of the horizontal error that F's printed survey tolerance (+-0.3 mm per axis) leaves at the reference point
       (the committed pre-check's F_A floor). The fit note adds nothing at the scored point for targets commanded
       as displacements from the end of the seating; one position step gives 0.16 mm horizontally.
   P, path (0.20). Owner: joint friction, the same factor and mutant as R (split only if the pre-check shows the
     damping half moving P by 0.1 alone). Window: each 1.16 s move.
     d_m, RMS distance from the timed straight reference over a move, mm. Floor 0.2 mm: one position step at each of the
       fourteen joints, 3-D shift of the reference point, root-sum-square, median 0.2009 mm (0.168 to 0.230).
   place (as 0.5 + 0.5 x place). Owner: setting down by feel (S within its survey tolerance). Mutant M-shelf: S at its drawn
     pose, lowering at 8 mm/s to the drawn height, then release. Window: T_e to T_e + 6.0 s.
     v_c, downward speed when the first foot meets S, mm/s. Floor 1.0 mm/s: a working value, ours, the
       pre-check's planning floor; one eighth of the reference's own lowering speed, not expected to bind.
     P_r, what S carries minus the tray's weight when the first gripper lets go, N. Floor 0.5 N: a working value, ours,
       the pre-check's planning floor; about three times what one torque-field step at the proximal joints
       of both arms produces at the tray. The audit never lifts it.
     d_s, horizontal distance and yaw from S's true seat mark. Expected to fail the 3x rule and be reported.
       Floor 2.0 mm (and 0.3 deg): the shelf's printed survey tolerance; by construction it cannot pass the 3x rule.
   Not band terms: completed (the chain, layers 1 and 2; M-int and M-grip are its event mutants) and J (layer 2; the dummies
   "never decline" and "decline all").
   The floor figures of H, R and P were computed on the maker's nominal model by the rule of section 2 (one field step at
   the observable): 93 targets, both arms at pitch -40 deg.
   The three place floors were fixed before any number of the built task existed: two working
   values, said so, and one tolerance.

7. EVENTS (each ends the unit's shift; the tray in work and every later tray score 0; earlier trays keep their scores)
   E1 module protection: coil over-temperature at the module's OT_Value (100 deg C on every module of version 1); driver
      stage at 120 deg C (the unit never produces it); TIMEOUT 0 in every record.
   E2 slip or drop: F_s > mu x F_c or F_n > F_c for 20 ms at a neck (mu printed "0.55 or more"; F_c = the finger stop's torque
      over 0.067 m, both pads together), armed 0.5 s after capture until release; or a tray released with its feet more than
      10 mm above a rest, or anywhere but over F or S.
   E3 spill: the deck more than 10 deg from level between capture and release.
   E4 contact: any contact other than pads on a grip block (not simulated), feet in F's cups or on S's top, and the two fingers
      of one hand with each other.
   E5 stop: an arm joint within 0.5 deg of an end of its range.
   Not events: a missed capture, a late or soft set-down, a declined tray, a press on the shelf.

8. WHAT EACH LINE OF THE PRE-CHECK TAKES OUT IF IT FAILS (quoted from the committed pre-check's rule, which holds where the two differ)
   (a) a1: layer 2 leaves the scored chain: protections stay events; J goes to a weight of 0.10; decline trays are kept only
       where a joint's holding torque exceeds its module's peak. a2: the maintainers shorten the lap or narrow the centre region
       before anything is built and the line is run again (the score's form is unchanged).
   (b) layer 1 leaves the scored chain; the internal force stays reported.
   (c) R is reported; the friction factor keeps P only if d_m passes the 3x rule under M-fric, otherwise the factor leaves the
       score (R and P out; Q renormalised over H and A).
   (d) A is reported (Q renormalised); the zero residual and the mounting stay in the units at the same tolerance.
   (e) one half fails: that quantity (P_r or v_c) is reported and place keeps the other; both fail: place leaves the product.
   With fewer than three of the five lines standing the form goes back to design.

9. GATES (only what makes scoring impossible or the run invalid; #88's)
   The controller imports and setup returns a valid mode choice inside its budget on every unit; the size cap; the static
   import audit; at least one call answered; at least 90 % of each unit's cycles on time; no write after the lock; no process
   left behind. A unit whose late time exceeds 10 % of its planned shift is stopped there.

10. REPORTED, NEVER SCORED
   Per tray: the internal force between the hands, the largest grasp-load share of capacity, moments at the welds, the grip
   torque by phase, J6's torque, every module's winding and reported temperatures and the smallest margin to a protection,
   stillness in each hold window, the largest tilt in the moves, d_s (if it fails the 3x rule), late cycles. Per unit: the kind
   and time of an event. Where the loop closes, unless the pre-check shows it moves a term of its own by 0.1 (M-loop). The zero
   ends at 0.25, 0.5 and 2 times each belief error.

11. FIXED BEFORE ANY BAND
   The reference's gains kp 400 / 400 / 300 / 300 / 60 / 60 / 60, kd 5 / 5 / 4 / 4 / 1.5 / 1.5 / 1.5; the
   label's margins: grasp load at most 0.80 of capacity, every winding at least 10 K below its protection.
