---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NEW TEXT:
  + HOW A ZERO-END MUTANT IS RUN AND READ (amendment 1).
  + (1) The mutant that places a zero end (M-load, M-fric, M-geom, M-shelf, each at its documents' value) is run once on
  : each of the three true units over the whole shift, with the trays' labels as the reference has them.
  + (2) It is run WITH THE GRASP TAKEN AS NEVER LIMITING: a breach of the capacity rule is recorded with its tray, hand
  : and time and does not end the run; the same for the spill angle and the stop margin, which are rules on the truth
  : channel and change nothing in the plant. The run ends at E1, at E4, at a dropped tray, at a step the plant cannot
  : integrate, or at the first cycle at which the mutant's law raises an exception or returns no frame; a missed capture
  : ends that tray's job only.
  + (3) Its rows are those of the trays it captured, read with a submission's windows, definitions and code.
  + (4) Z_x is the median of x over those rows pooled over the three units (the instrument's median: the value at index
  : n // 2 of the sorted rows; the 90th percentile is the nearest rank). It EXISTS only if those rows number at least
  : half of the reference's rows of x and at least two units each give at least one tenth of their reference rows.
  + (5) A quantity whose zero end does not exist is reported only, with the recorded reason. Its factor stays in the
  : score through completed_i.
  + (6) On the frozen set nothing is changed to make a zero end exist or a quantity pass.
  + (An entry added to section 10.) With the frozen set, per zero-end mutant and unit: every breach of the capacity
  : rule, of the spill angle and of the stop margin that its run recorded, with its tray, hand and time; and for a
  : quantity whose zero end does not exist, the recorded reason.

READ AS:

    shelf as its site has it (item 5).
  - (2) "The spill angle" is the condition of E3 and "the stop margin" the condition of E5. "A dropped tray" is the
    second half of E2 (a tray released with its feet more than 10 mm above a rest, or anywhere but over F or S). The

  - (3) Of a tray whose job the run did not finish, the rows are those whose windows had run to their end before the run

  - (4) "The reference's rows of x" are the rows of the full end F_x: the reference's rows of x on the three units,
    pooled. "Their reference rows" are, for one unit, the reference's rows of x on that unit. The nearest-rank
    percentile is the one of section 2's full end and of item 1's T_x.
  - (5) The recorded reason says, per unit, how the mutant's run ended and how many rows of x it gave.
  - The second sentence of section 3 stays: the medians at 0.25, 0.5 and 2 times a belief's error are recorded beside
    Z_x and are never an end. They are run and read as (1) to (3) say. The maintainers' words do not speak of them; this sentence

RECORDED WITH (5), the maintainers' words: "This DEPARTS from the first addendum's sentence that a factor is not taken out
for biting harder: no band can be placed from rows that do not exist; the factor stays in the score through

NOTE OF THE MAINTAINERS, not a sentence of the rule: the supervisor and the law get the missed-capture branch before

NEW TEXT (section 4 in full):
  + 4. THE 3x RULE (amendment 1)
  + A quantity PASSES the 3x rule when its zero end exists (section 3 as amended) and its Z_x is at least 3 F_x. A
  : quantity whose Z_x is below 3 F_x is merged or reported only. Merging is possible only inside a term: in H, e_z with
  : theta. A quantity of R, A or P that does not pass is reported, and its term leaves Q. A quantity whose zero end does

  + Merged: its credit factor is dropped and the term is computed from the owner's remaining quantity (H keeps the other
  : of e_z and theta). A term of Q left with no scored quantity is reported only and its weight goes to the remaining
  : terms of Q in proportion; if no term of Q is left, Q_i = 1.
  + The place term has one quantity that can be scored, the press P_r. If the press does not pass, place_i = 1: the
  : place term leaves the product (tray_i = completed_i x V_i x Q_i).
  + Each quantity is decided ONCE, on the frozen set, by this rule, with the floors and the zero ends as amended; never
  : by looking at a model's episode. H, R, A and P keep their quantities, owners, zero-end mutants and weights of
  : record. Nothing is taken out before the frozen set exists.
  + Every outcome of the 3x rule gives a defined score; no outcome sends the form back, and no unit is drawn again
  : because of one.

  - "Nothing is taken out before the frozen set exists" is the maintainers' "Nothing is taken out now: A, R, P and the tilt

PROMPTED BY: the development units' figures (against the provisional full ends). Only the height error under M-load

ITEM 4. THE TRAY'S FORMULA, V AND Q_i
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AMENDS section 5. In its block of formula lines this line is replaced:
  AS COMMITTED, BAND_RULE.txt line 34:
  > tray_i = completed_i x Q_i x (0.5 + 0.5 x place_i)

Fields: `json:structure.tray`, `json:structure.Q`, `json:renormalisation`.

NEW TEXT (the formula lines of section 5 as they stand from this amendment on, then the definitions that are new):
  + Q_i     = (sum over the terms T of Q that pass of w_T x T) / W, with w_T the weight of record of term T (w_H 0.30,
  : w_R 0.25, w_A 0.25, w_P 0.20) and W the sum of w_T over the terms that pass; Q_i = 1 if no term passes. Each term T
  : is the mean of its rows over tray i's job.
  + H row   = L(e_z) x L(theta), one row per hold. (As committed. If one of the two does not pass, the row is L of the
  : other: section 4.)
  + P row   = L(d_m) x progress_m, one row per move; progress = travel projected on the move, in [0, 1]. (As committed.)
  + V_i     = the share of the job's holds that are VISITED.
  + place_i = 0.5 + 0.5 x L(P_r) if the press passes, otherwise 1. (Item 5.)
  + tray_i  = completed_i x V_i x Q_i x place_i
  + unit_u  = 0.85 x the mean over the unit's CARRY trays of tray_i + 0.15 x J_u. (As committed.)
  + raw     = gates x the mean over the three units of unit_u; reward = min(1, raw / anchor). (As committed.)
  + completed_i: as committed (section 5).
  + V_i, THE JOB FOLLOWED: the share of the job's holds that are VISITED. Let p_k be the mean position of the tray's
  : reference point over the scored window of hold k (the last 0.4 s) and t_k its target; p_(-1) the reference point at
  : the carry's start and t_(-1) the lifted point L. Hold k is VISITED when the 3-D distance |p_k - t_k| is at most
  : 0.020 m AND |(p_k - p_(k-1)) - (t_k - t_(k-1))| is at most 0.020 m. 0.020 m is less than half of the shortest move
  : any job contains (0.0447 m, a fixed join of the lap; every job's last fixed move is 0.0566 m; a drawn move is at
  : least 0.06 m) and less than the smallest distance between two targets of the grid (0.030 m): a tray that stays where
  : it was between two holds is never credited. The definition up to "... is at most 0.020 m." is printed in the
  : acceptance specification; the sentence that begins "0.020 m is less than half" is not.
  + Q_i: H, R, A and P keep their quantities, owners, zero-end mutants and weights of record. Each quantity is decided
  : ONCE, on the frozen set, by the committed 3x rule (section 4 as amended); the weights are renormalised over the
  : terms that pass; if none passes, Q_i = 1.
  + When a term leaves Q (3x rule) the remaining Q weights are divided by their sum; if no term remains, Q_i = 1.
From the words "the share of the job's holds" to its end, the paragraph of V_i is the maintainers' text, word for word

HOW A UNIT'S SCORE IS COMPUTED UNDER THE RULE AS AMENDED
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The steps are in order. Each names in square brackets the definition it uses: a section of the committed rule, an item
of this amendment or an entry of NAMES. Step
0 is done once for the frozen set. Steps 1 to 10 are done for one unit. Step 11 joins the three units.

THE RECORD OF A UNIT'S RUN holds: the shift sheet (per slot the kind of job and its n targets t_0 to t_(n-1)); the
frozen set's label of each tray (carry or decline); the unit's site (F's and S's true poses, S's seat mark) and each
tray's true total mass and drawing (feet size and positions); per cycle the controller's reply (in time or late; the
decline flag); and per step, from the truth channel: the tray's full pose (its reference point and orientation), what F
and S carry of the tray, each hand's clamp F_c and the loads F_s and F_n at its neck, the instants at which a hand
captured and released, every module's status, every contact by class, every arm joint's distance to its stops, and
whether the plant integrated the step.

STEP 0. THE ENDS AND THE FORM OF THE SCORE: once, on the frozen set, before any submission is graded.
  0.1 floor_x of every quantity: its committed floor [section 6]; for e_z, theta, e_xy and d_m the value in
      rules/band_rule_amendment_1_floors.json at the digest above [item 1].
  0.2 F_x = max(floor_x, 1.25 x the 90th percentile of x over the reference's rows on the three units, pooled) [section
      2; NAMES for the percentile]. The rows of the press are the reference's set-downs of every carry tray [item 5].
  0.3 Z_x: each zero-end mutant is run and read as item 2 says. Z_x is the median of its pooled rows, if it exists
      [section 3; item 2].
  0.4 A quantity passes if Z_x exists and Z_x is at least 3 F_x [item 3]. That fixes the form of the score, the same for
      every unit and tray:
      - H is a term of Q if e_z or theta passes. Its row is L(e_z) x L(theta) if both pass, and L of the one that passes
        if one does.
      - R is a term of Q if rho_t passes; A if e_xy passes; P if d_m passes.
      - The weight of a term of Q is its weight of record (0.30 H, 0.25 R, 0.25 A, 0.20 P) divided by the sum of the
        weights of record of the terms of Q. With no term of Q, Q_i = 1.
      - place_i holds the press if P_r passes and the audit has found S's height shown. Otherwise place_i = 1 [item 5].
  0.5 The anchor is the reference's raw score on the frozen set: steps 2 to 11 for the reference, with gates = 1, up to
      raw [section 5].

STEP 1. THE GATES [section 9]. gates = 1 if every gate holds for the submission, otherwise 0. A unit whose late time
        exceeds 10 % of its planned shift is stopped there.

STEP 2. WHERE THE UNIT'S SHIFT ENDS. It ends at the first of these: an event E1 to E5 [section 7; E2 as item 8 has it];
        a step that the plant cannot integrate [item 11]; the stop for late time [section 9]; the end of the last slot.
        The tray in work is the tray of the slot in which the shift ends. An outcome decided before the end of the shift
        is kept: a carry tray's completed_i decided at T_e + 6.0, a decline tray DECLINED at its put-back deadline, and
        the carried lap's first hold VISITED at the end of its scored window. Every outcome not decided by then is lost:
        completed_i = 0, not DECLINED, and the first hold not VISITED. This holds for an event, for a step the plant
        cannot integrate and for the stop for late time [section 7; item 11].

STEP 3. WHAT HAPPENED TO EACH TRAY, in the order of the slots. For tray i:
  - whether it was reached, that is presented before the shift ended;
  - whether each hand captured its neck by t = 5.0 s [NAMES]. If not, and no decline
    was sent before t = 5.0, the capture is missed and the job has failed; that is no event [section 7];
  - whether a decline was sent, and its time t_d [item 6];
  - whether an event or the end of the shift fell inside its slot [step 2].

STEP 4. completed_i of a carry tray [section 5]. It is 1 if the tray was captured, carried without an event, and stands
        on S inside its seat frame (the seat mark grown by 10 mm on the true shelf, all four feet, each with its bottom
        within 1 mm of S's top) with both grippers open at T_e + 6.0 [NAMES: "stands on S inside its seat frame", "both
        grippers open"]. Otherwise it is 0. A carry tray for which a decline was sent, a carry tray missed at capture,
        cut by an event or by the end of the shift, or never reached has completed_i = 0.

STEP 5. V_i of a carry tray with completed_i = 1 [item 4]. For k = 0, ..., n - 1, p_k is the mean position of the tray's
        reference point over the last 0.4 s of hold k. p_(-1) is the tray's reference point in the record of the last
        step before the carry's first cycle (the state at t = 9.0 s), one reading, not a window mean, and t_(-1) is L.
        Hold k is VISITED if |p_k - t_k| is at most 0.020 m and |(p_k - p_(k-1)) - (t_k - t_(k-1))| is at most 0.020 m.
        V_i is the number of VISITED holds divided by n.

STEP 6. Q_i of a carry tray with completed_i = 1 [item 4; section 1 for L; section 6 for the quantities and their
        windows; step 0 for the ends and the form]. With no term of Q, Q_i = 1. Otherwise:
  - H, one row per hold k: e_z is the absolute difference in height between p_k and t_k; theta is the mean over the
    scored window of the angle of the deck from level. The row is L(e_z) x L(theta), or L of the one that passes.
  - R, one row per target that the job's list reaches at least twice from directions of arrival t_k - t_(k-1), with
    t_(-1) = L, at least 60 deg apart: rho_t is the largest distance between the p_k of all that target's holds. The row
    is L(rho_t).
  - A, one row per hold k: e_xy is the horizontal distance between p_k and t_k. The row is L(e_xy).
  - P, one row per move k: d_m is the RMS distance of the reference point from the timed straight reference from t_(k-1)
    to t_k, with path length by the trapezoid of equal thirds over 1.16 s; progress_m is the travel at the
    move's end projected on the move over its length, clipped to [0, 1]. The row is L(d_m) x progress_m.
  - Each term of Q is the mean of its rows over the job. Q_i is the sum over the terms of Q of the term times its weight
    of step 0.4.

STEP 7. place_i of a carry tray with completed_i = 1 [item 5]. If the press does not pass, or the audit found S's height
        not shown, place_i = 1. Otherwise P_r is the size of the difference between what S carries and the tray's weight
        in the cycle of item 5; if S carries less than 2 N in that cycle, L(P_r) = 0; and place_i = 0.5 + 0.5 x L(P_r).

STEP 8. tray_i = completed_i x V_i x Q_i x place_i [item 4]. It is 0 for every carry tray with completed_i = 0.

STEP 9. J_u [item 6].
  - A tray is DECLINED if its slot's capture was not missed (step 3), both hands captured it in its slot before the
    decline was sent, and at t_d + 4.0 s it stands released in F with its four feet on F's plate inside their cups [item
    6; NAMES: "stands in F", "both grippers open"].
  - TPR is the number of the unit's decline trays that are DECLINED, divided by the number of its decline trays. (Every
    unit of the frozen set has one or two decline trays; a candidate on which any one of its decline trays
    has no draw that passes the generator's check for a decline tray within 64 draws is rejected before the freeze.)
  - FPR is 0 if the first hold of the carried lap's job is VISITED (the test of step 5 for k = 0), and 1 if it is not,
    whether or not a decline was sent for that tray and also when the tray was never reached.
  - J_u = max(0, TPR - FPR).

STEP 10. unit_u = 0.85 x the mean of tray_i over the unit's carry trays + 0.15 x J_u [section 5]. Every carry tray of
         the unit is in the mean, also one that was never reached.

STEP 11. raw = gates x the mean of unit_u over the three units. reward = min(1, raw / anchor) [section 5].
