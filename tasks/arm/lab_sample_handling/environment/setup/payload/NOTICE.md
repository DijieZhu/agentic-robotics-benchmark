# NOTICE - sources

- **The robot** is the Franka Panda model of robosuite 1.5.2 (MIT), installed unmodified in the task image.
- **Everything else on the bench** (the tubes, the racks, the centrifuge, its rotor and lid, the scale) is built from
  MuJoCo primitives and one generated mesh; no third-party assets.
- **The room in the cameras' pictures** (wall benches, shelves, glassware, floor, ceiling lights) is a bay of the
  Unity Asset Store pack "Archviz Laboratory Pack", simplified; its light was measured in a path-traced render of
  that bay under Poly Haven's `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky` sky (CC0).
