# NOTICE - sources

- **The robot** is the Franka Panda model of robosuite 1.5.2 (MIT), installed unmodified in the task image;
  `box_transport_env.py` loads its files.
- **Everything else on the bench** (the tubes, the racks, the centrifuge, its rotor and lid) is built from MuJoCo
  primitives and one generated mesh in `lab_sim.py`; no third-party assets.
