# NOTICE - sources

- **The robot** is the Franka Panda model of robosuite 1.5.2 (MIT), installed unmodified in the task image;
  `shelf_sim.py` loads its files.
- **Everything else** (the gondola bay, the cart and tote, the products and their printed looks) is built from
  MuJoCo primitives in `shelf_sim.py` and `shelf_world.py`; no third-party assets.
