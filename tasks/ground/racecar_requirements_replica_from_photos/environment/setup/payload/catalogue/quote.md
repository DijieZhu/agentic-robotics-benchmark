## Fixed items of every car

The rolling chassis kit AVH-YTXE (data page DS-01) and the deck kit (deck plate, short standoffs,
antenna mount and antennas, rear wing frame, spacer block) are supplied together, one set for every car, at
301.52 USD for each set.

## Terms

- Item code is the code to order by. Purchase line is the line of the bill of materials that the item belongs
  to. Data page is the maker's datasheet of the item.
- Fit and Connector are the fit class and the connector class of the item, where it has any.
- Arrival lot is the lot that the item is delivered from. The lots listed are those in stock at the date of this
  quotation.
- Per car is the number of pieces of the item on a car. Pack of is the number of pieces in a pack.
- Prices are in USD, for a pack as listed.
