# The picture manual

Twenty photographs of this station and this robot, `photo_01.jpg` to `photo_20.jpg`, with
`photos.json`: per photograph its caption, its size in pixels and the scale references in view. A caption is
short, says what its photograph shows and carries no dimension. The poses of the camera and its lens are not known.

Every photograph shows something of known size: a datum board of the cell, a steel rule, or parts whose sizes the
drawings or the mesh files give. The finished robot in the photographs carries no interface screws, as in the cell.

The photographs show the cell, the base, the modules, the interfaces close up, moments of a build and the finished
robot. They show what is; they do not prescribe how the work is done. The cell fastens a module whenever its lock
condition holds, whatever is mounted already; whether a way of working can be carried out is for you to establish.
